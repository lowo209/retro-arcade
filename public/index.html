YOUR ARCADE, ON YOUR TERMS

A little setup.
A lot of play.

Everything runs on your computer. Bring your own game files, connect a controller, and settle in.

01

Bring the originals

Add your legally obtained ROMs, disc images and PS1 firmware to the private folders. The setup script handles the supplied UMK3 files.

02

Make yourself comfortable

Use a keyboard or connect a USB / Bluetooth controller. Configure both players from the emulator’s controls menu.

03

Keep your progress

Use the emulator’s save menu and export a backup. Browser saves stay on this browser and this local address.

WELCOME BACK, PLAYER.

Your own little arcade.

Retro Arcade is a local library for playing original games through EmulatorJS and, experimentally, PS2 Web Recomp. No game recreations. No game data is included in the source.

Game availability comes from your local files. “Ready to launch” means setup checks passed; it does not certify gameplay, sound or multiplayer. GT4 remains experimental.

Your supplied cover images are used when installed locally, with illustrated fallbacks for the source-only edition. The platform badges identify the actual game versions. Game names and cover artwork belong to their respective owners.

# Local cover-art sources

Added 24 October 2026 for the three newly supplied European PS1 games. These are original retail **PAL front-cover scans**, retrieved from LaunchBox Games Database's **Box - Front (Europe)** category. Its fan-art, reconstructed-box, Japanese and North American entries were not selected. Each downloaded image was opened and visually checked for the exact game title, original PlayStation branding and PAL mark.

The JPEG bytes are retained unchanged under ignored `private/covers/`, with their correct `.jpg` extension. These artwork files are excluded from Git and the source-only export; they are not covered by this website's MIT licence. The existing user-supplied covers, including RE3 and GT2, are untouched.

| Game | Private file | Dimensions | Source page | Original image |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ace Combat 3: Electrosphere | `private/covers/ac3.jpg` | 1000 × 1020 | [LaunchBox image gallery](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/11133-ace-combat-3-electrosphere) | [PAL front JPEG](https://images.launchbox-app.com/c72b5ecf-a420-4745-be51-c396d1dbbbfd.jpg) |
| Ghost in the Shell | `private/covers/gits.jpg` | 640 × 640 | [LaunchBox image gallery](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/395917-ghost-in-the-shell) | [PAL front JPEG](https://images.launchbox-app.com/f9f7f9c2-6eae-4213-875c-5bc5fe69edde.jpg) |
| G-Police: Weapons of Justice | `private/covers/gpolice2.jpg` | 951 × 960 | [LaunchBox image gallery](https://gamesdb.launchbox-app.com/games/images/12774-g-police-weapons-of-justice) | [PAL front JPEG](https://images.launchbox-app.com/fc7a540d-9f95-4fee-8a7c-2f11593b9ca6.jpg) |

The G-Police image is explicitly **Weapons of Justice**, the sequel, rather than the first G-Police. The Ghost in the Shell scan is the PS1 Fuchikoma game, rather than a later Stand Alone Complex release. The Ace Combat scan is the European fighter-jet artwork, rather than the different Japanese or US packaging.
